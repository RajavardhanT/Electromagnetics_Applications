# Quantum Technologies

At atomic and quantum scales, electromagnetic fields interact with quantized energy levels and coherent quantum states. This section connects classical field quantities to quantum interactions and sensing.

## Planned topics

- Atom-field interaction
- Electric-dipole interaction
- Rabi frequency
- AC Stark effect
- Autler-Townes splitting
- Rydberg electrometry
- Atomic RF receivers
- Quantum magnetometry
- Microwave-optical conversion
- Cavity QED
- Floquet engineering of driven atoms

A recurring bridge is

$$
\mathbf{E}(t)
\rightarrow
-\mathbf{d}\cdot\mathbf{E}
\rightarrow
\Omega=\frac{\mathbf{d}\cdot\mathbf{E}}{\hbar}
\rightarrow
\text{quantum-state response}
\rightarrow
\text{measurement}.
$$
