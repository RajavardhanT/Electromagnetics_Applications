# Electromagnetic Boundary Conditions

Boundary conditions describe how electromagnetic fields behave when they encounter an interface between two materials.

They follow directly from Maxwell's equations and are essential for understanding reflection, refraction, waveguides, transmission lines, antennas, resonators, shielding, and optical systems.

## Tangential electric field

$$
\hat{n}\times(\mathbf{E}_2-\mathbf{E}_1)=0
$$

for an interface without an impressed magnetic surface current.

## Tangential magnetic field

$$
\hat{n}\times(\mathbf{H}_2-\mathbf{H}_1)=\mathbf{J}_s.
$$

## Normal electric flux density

$$
\hat{n}\cdot(\mathbf{D}_2-\mathbf{D}_1)=\rho_s.
$$

## Normal magnetic flux density

$$
\hat{n}\cdot(\mathbf{B}_2-\mathbf{B}_1)=0.
$$
