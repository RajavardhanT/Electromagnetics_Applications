# Electromagnetic Waves

## Overview

Time-varying electric and magnetic fields can sustain one another and propagate through space as electromagnetic waves.

## Wave equation

In a homogeneous, source-free, linear medium,

$$
\nabla^2\mathbf{E}-\mu\epsilon\frac{\partial^2\mathbf{E}}{\partial t^2}=0,
$$

with an analogous equation for $\mathbf{H}$.

The phase velocity is

$$
v=\frac{1}{\sqrt{\mu\epsilon}}.
$$

In free space,

$$
c=\frac{1}{\sqrt{\mu_0\epsilon_0}}.
$$

## Why it matters

Electromagnetic-wave propagation is the physical basis of radio, microwave links, radar, satellite communications, optical systems, and many sensing technologies.
