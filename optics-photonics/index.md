# Optics & Photonics

Optics is the high-frequency regime of electromagnetism. Maxwell's equations therefore provide a direct bridge between classical electromagnetic theory and photonic technologies.

## Planned topics

- Reflection and refraction
- Fresnel equations
- Interference and diffraction
- Optical fibers
- Lasers
- Waveguides
- Photonic crystals
- Integrated photonics
- Plasmonics and nanophotonics
