# Computational Electromagnetics

Many practical electromagnetic systems require numerical solutions of Maxwell's equations in complex geometries and materials.

## Planned topics

- Finite-element method (FEM)
- Finite-difference time-domain (FDTD)
- Method of moments (MoM)
- Eigenmode analysis
- Frequency-domain simulation
- Time-domain simulation
- Meshing and convergence
- Boundary conditions and absorbing boundaries
- HFSS, CST, and open-source tools
