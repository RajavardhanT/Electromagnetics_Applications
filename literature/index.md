# Electromagnetics Literature Guide

This page groups sources by **domain and source type** so that foundational literature, modern reviews, representative experiments, institutional resources, and metrology sources remain easy to locate.

## General electromagnetics

### Open university and institutional resources

- [MIT OpenCourseWare — Electromagnetics and Applications](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-013-electromagnetics-and-applications-spring-2009/) — fields through communication systems, antennas, sensors, motors and optics.
- [MIT OpenCourseWare — graduate Electromagnetics](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-630-electromagnetics-fall-2006/) — advanced EM with demonstrations and computational material.

## Radar

- [MIT Lincoln Laboratory — Introduction to Radar Systems](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-ll-001-introduction-to-radar-systems-spring-2007/) — open lecture series covering radar-system concepts and technologies.

## Quantum optics and atom–field interaction

- M. Fleischhauer, A. Imamoglu and J. P. Marangos, “Electromagnetically induced transparency,” *Rev. Mod. Phys.* **77**, 633 (2005). https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.77.633

## Rydberg atoms and RF sensing

- J. A. Sedlacek et al., “Microwave electrometry with Rydberg atoms in a vapour cell using bright atomic resonances,” *Nature Physics* **8**, 819–824 (2012). https://doi.org/10.1038/nphys2423
- C. L. Holloway et al., work on atom-based RF electric-field metrology and Rydberg EIT/Autler–Townes sensing.
- NIST Electromagnetic Fields Group resources: https://www.nist.gov/ctl/rf-technology-division/electromagnetic-fields-group

## Atomic magnetometry

- D. Budker and M. Romalis, “Optical Magnetometry,” *Nature Physics* **3**, 227–234 (2007). https://doi.org/10.1038/nphys566
- W. Happer, “Optical Pumping,” *Rev. Mod. Phys.* **44**, 169 (1972). https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.44.169
- I. K. Kominis et al., “A subfemtotesla multichannel atomic magnetometer,” *Nature* **422**, 596–599 (2003). https://doi.org/10.1038/nature01484

## Radiation and particle detectors

- W. Shockley, “Currents to Conductors Induced by a Moving Point Charge,” *J. Appl. Phys.* **9**, 635–636 (1938).
- S. Ramo, “Currents Induced by Electron Motion,” *Proc. IRE* **27**, 584–585 (1939).

## Spacecraft, EMC and metrology

- [NASA JSC — EMI/EMC](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/jsc-emi-emc/) — spacecraft EMC engineering, simulation and test.
- [NIST — Electromagnetic Field Strength Metrology](https://www.nist.gov/programs-projects/electromagnetic-field-strength-metrology) — SI-linked field-strength calibration and EMC measurement science.
- [NIST Electromagnetic Fields Group](https://www.nist.gov/ctl/rf-technology-division/electromagnetic-fields-group) — antenna, radiated-field and quantum-based field metrology.
