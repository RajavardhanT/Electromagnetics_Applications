# Electromagnetics Literature Guide

This page groups sources by **domain and source type** so that foundational literature, textbooks, modern reviews, representative experiments, and institutional resources remain easy to locate.

## General electromagnetics

### Classic / advanced texts

- D. J. Griffiths, *Introduction to Electrodynamics* — clear physical development.
- J. D. Jackson, *Classical Electrodynamics* — advanced classical theory.
- D. K. Cheng, *Field and Wave Electromagnetics* — engineering-oriented field/wave treatment.
- R. F. Harrington, *Time-Harmonic Electromagnetic Fields* — rigorous engineering EM.
- E. C. Jordan and K. G. Balmain, *Electromagnetic Waves and Radiating Systems* — fields, waves and radiation.

### Open university resources

- [MIT OpenCourseWare — Electromagnetics and Applications](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-013-electromagnetics-and-applications-spring-2009/) — fields through communication systems, antennas, sensors, motors and optics.
- [MIT OpenCourseWare — graduate Electromagnetics](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-630-electromagnetics-fall-2006/) — advanced EM with demonstrations and computational material.

## RF and microwave

- D. M. Pozar, *Microwave Engineering* — practical and theoretical microwave reference.
- R. E. Collin, *Foundations for Microwave Engineering* — rigorous microwave foundations.
- G. Gonzalez, *Microwave Transistor Amplifiers* — active RF design.
- H. A. Wheeler and classic transmission-line/network literature for historical engineering perspective.

## Antennas and arrays

- C. A. Balanis, *Antenna Theory: Analysis and Design* — broad modern antenna reference.
- R. C. Hansen, *Phased Array Antennas* — array design and scan behavior.
- R. J. Mailloux, *Phased Array Antenna Handbook* — practical phased-array reference.
- S. Silver (ed.), *Microwave Antenna Theory and Design* — classic radiation/aperture treatment.

## Radar

- M. I. Skolnik, *Introduction to Radar Systems* — classic radar systems reference.
- M. A. Richards, *Fundamentals of Radar Signal Processing* — signal-processing-centric modern reference.
- [MIT Lincoln Laboratory — Introduction to Radar Systems](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-ll-001-introduction-to-radar-systems-spring-2007/) — open lecture series.

## Numerical electromagnetics

- A. Taflove and S. C. Hagness, *Computational Electrodynamics: The Finite-Difference Time-Domain Method*.
- J. Jin, *The Finite Element Method in Electromagnetics*.
- R. F. Harrington, *Field Computation by Moment Methods*.
- W. C. Chew, *Waves and Fields in Inhomogeneous Media*.

## Optics and nonlinear optics

- B. E. A. Saleh and M. C. Teich, *Fundamentals of Photonics*.
- R. W. Boyd, *Nonlinear Optics*.
- M. Born and E. Wolf, *Principles of Optics*.

## Quantum optics and atom–field interaction

- C. Cohen-Tannoudji, J. Dupont-Roc and G. Grynberg, *Atom–Photon Interactions*.
- M. O. Scully and M. S. Zubairy, *Quantum Optics*.
- L. Allen and J. H. Eberly, *Optical Resonance and Two-Level Atoms*.
- M. Fleischhauer, A. Imamoglu and J. P. Marangos, “Electromagnetically induced transparency,” *Rev. Mod. Phys.* **77**, 633 (2005). https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.77.633

## Rydberg atoms and RF sensing

- T. F. Gallagher, *Rydberg Atoms* — foundational monograph.
- J. A. Sedlacek et al., “Microwave electrometry with Rydberg atoms in a vapour cell using bright atomic resonances,” *Nature Physics* **8**, 819–824 (2012). https://doi.org/10.1038/nphys2423
- C. L. Holloway et al., work on atom-based RF electric-field metrology and Rydberg EIT/Autler–Townes sensing.
- NIST Electromagnetic Fields Group resources: https://www.nist.gov/ctl/rf-technology-division/electromagnetic-fields-group

## Atomic magnetometry

- D. Budker and M. Romalis, “Optical Magnetometry,” *Nature Physics* **3**, 227–234 (2007). https://doi.org/10.1038/nphys566
- D. Budker and D. F. J. Kimball, *Optical Magnetometry*.
- W. Happer, “Optical Pumping,” *Rev. Mod. Phys.* **44**, 169 (1972). https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.44.169
- I. K. Kominis et al., “A subfemtotesla multichannel atomic magnetometer,” *Nature* **422**, 596–599 (2003). https://doi.org/10.1038/nature01484

## Radiation and particle detectors

- G. F. Knoll, *Radiation Detection and Measurement*.
- H. Spieler, *Semiconductor Detector Systems*.
- W. Shockley, “Currents to Conductors Induced by a Moving Point Charge,” *J. Appl. Phys.* **9**, 635–636 (1938).
- S. Ramo, “Currents Induced by Electron Motion,” *Proc. IRE* **27**, 584–585 (1939).

## Spacecraft, EMC and metrology

- [NASA JSC — EMI/EMC](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/jsc-emi-emc/) — spacecraft EMC engineering, simulation and test.
- [NIST — Electromagnetic Field Strength Metrology](https://www.nist.gov/programs-projects/electromagnetic-field-strength-metrology) — SI-linked field-strength calibration and EMC measurement science.
- [NIST Electromagnetic Fields Group](https://www.nist.gov/ctl/rf-technology-division/electromagnetic-fields-group) — antenna, radiated-field and quantum-based field metrology.
